# Manual WhatsApp confirmation and automatic visit emails

The selected flow uses a WhatsApp booking contact and a separate, consented follow-up email. After owner approval, the team opens the confirmation draft in CRM and presses Send in WhatsApp. WATI is removed from the active to-do list; this flow does not send through a WhatsApp API.

For these bookings, the email worker also sends an automatic email confirmation, sends the reminder in the hour before the visit, and requests attendance and feedback from two hours afterward. After matching customer and property advisor attendance reports, one additional email addresses the customer's interest or missed visit, assistance needs, and options to reschedule or explore more properties. It is eligible for seven days after the visit and is deduplicated per visit/time. Customer responses through the private response page update the CRM. Ordinary email or WhatsApp replies are not automatically interpreted.

This change adds no paid messaging service. Email provider sending limits and existing computer/hosting availability still apply. The hosted sender is propertynm38@gmail.com. Local tests use simulated delivery; hosted checks and the separate real sender test are recorded below.

The visit worker sends opted-in email contacts:

- A confirmation on its next run after the owner confirms a future visit.
- A reminder within the hour before the visit, unless a confirmation sent within that hour already covers it.
- A feedback request from two hours after the visit until one day afterward.

Cancelled, unconfirmed, past, and nonconsenting bookings do not receive confirmations. Existing confirmed future visits with email consent are also eligible on the first run. Rescheduled visits become eligible after owner approval of the new time. Confirmation, reminder, and feedback each have a unique outbox record per visit/time to prevent duplicate submissions. Uncertain SMTP failures are held as `needs_review` rather than automatically resent.

The owner dashboard's Visit responses and advisor requests table shows confirmation, reminder, and review email statuses. `sent` means the SMTP server accepted the message; it does not guarantee inbox delivery. `needs_review` or a persistent `sending` state requires checking provider records before any retry.

## Independent site attendance

Mira Studio → CRM → Load / refresh CRM → Visit attendance and needs attention shows customer and advisor reports separately, conflicts, property/date totals and missing confirmations. Load advisor register controls, select a confirmed property/date, enter the assigned advisor name and create a private register link. Share it manually only with that advisor. The link permits attendance updates for that property/date, exposes customer names and visit times only, expires seven days after the date and can be revoked. It is a bearer link, not an authenticated advisor identity; records identify the assigned link holder and remain advisor-reported evidence. No-show entry is allowed only two hours after the visit. Corrections are recorded in the journey event log.

Both parties report actual attendance after the appointment. Matching Yes reports permit the interest/assistance email; matching No reports permit the missed-visit email. Missing or conflicting reports hold outcome emails and appear in the attention list. The neutral customer feedback email still requests the customer's report. Reports do not infer attendance from a promise or silence. Previously sent email cannot be recalled after a correction. Outcome follow-ups expire seven days after the visit.

Emails contain secure action links for attendance, rescheduling, cancellation, feedback and pausing. Opening a link does not save a response; the customer submits the form. Not buying now and Pause visit emails stop this booking's visit emails without cancelling its appointment or unrelated email subscriptions. Resume requires an explicit form submission. Explore other properties restores saved preferences and requested changes in Mira and displays matching alternatives excluding the original property; matches depend on the current listing data.

WhatsApp remains manual. Optional post-visit drafts require separately verified WhatsApp permission. Conflicting attendance and paused follow-ups block those visit drafts. No WhatsApp API is activated.
